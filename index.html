Kit of parts, not modular

Everything you need.
Nothing pre-assembled.

  1. FoundationA standard slab, poured on your site while the kit is made.
  2. Floor & interiorFinished floor and partitions lay out the rooms.
  3. Steel frameA SidePlate® moment frame or steel shear walls, depending on the series. Bolted, with no field welds.
  4. Wall panelsInsulated steel panels, up to R-35, fix to the frame.
  5. Glazing & doorsWindows and doors set into the framed openings.
  6. RoofInsulated roof, skylights and rooftop HVAC.
01

Ships on standard trucks

Flat-packed parts need no wide loads, escorts or oversize permits, and nothing gets damaged in transit.

02

Clear spans set by series

Spans aren't limited by a truck's width. Moment-frame series open up to 100' for gyms and halls; shear-wall series suit classroom rows. See the table below.

03

Your contractor, or ours

Any qualified GC bolts it together, with no factory queue and deliveries in build order.

04

Built for future expansion
