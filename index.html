Kit of parts, not modular

Everything you need.
Nothing pre-assembled.

Modular buildings ship as finished boxes. CUUBE ships as a complete kit of engineered steel parts that are built into one permanent building on your site.

01

Ships on standard trucks

Parts ship flat-packed on standard flatbeds, not as wide-load boxes that often need oversize permits, escorts and route restrictions.

02

Nothing to damage in transit

There are no finished walls, plumbing or wiring bouncing down the highway. Every component is inspected and arrives as built, so you aren't repairing a building before it opens.

03

Not limited by a truck's width

Modules are sized to fit on a road, which sets your room sizes and seams. A kit bolts into one structure, so you get clear spans up to 100' for gyms and halls with no seams between rooms.

04

Delivered in build order

Just-in-time, phased deliveries arrive as each stage is ready. Staging stays small and campuses can stay open.

05

Complete and pre-verified

The frame, panels, roof, doors, windows, HVAC, fans, acoustic deck and daylighting all come from a standardized, pre-sourced catalog that matches DSA pre-approved plans.

06

Your contractor, or ours

Any pre-qualified general contractor can assemble the bolt-together frame, or KYA can build it for you. You aren't tied to a factory's schedule.
