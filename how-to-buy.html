Questions.

Is CUUBE modular?

No. Modular buildings are built as boxes in a factory and trucked to the site. CUUBE is a complete kit of pre-engineered steel parts. It ships flat on standard trucks and is bolted together on your site into one permanent building, with no oversize loads and no seams between modules.

Is CUUBE a portable building?

No. CUUBE is a permanent steel building on a permanent foundation, with a 100+ year lifespan. It is not a relocatable classroom.

Is it DSA approved?

Yes. CUUBE architectural drawings are DSA pre-approved, which reduces the inspections and verifications needed for your project.

How long does it really take?

Most projects take 9–12 months from design to finish line, up to 50% faster than a traditional build. Construction itself can take as little as four months in ideal weather and site conditions.
