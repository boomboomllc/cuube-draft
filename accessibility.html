Accessibility statement

We want everyone, including public agency staff and community members, to be able to use this site.

Our standard

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA [confirm after audit].

What we do

Semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard navigation with visible focus, a skip link, text alternatives for images, and sufficient color contrast. Animation respects your device's reduced-motion setting, and the building assembly shows a still version on small screens and slow connections.
