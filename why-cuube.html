Kit vs. modular

Why we ship parts,
not boxes.

Modular construction builds rooms in a factory and trucks them to the site. CUUBE takes a different route: a pre-engineered kit of parts, built on site into one permanent steel structure.

CUUBE kit of partsModular building
What arrivesEngineered steel parts, flat-packedFinished room-sized boxes
TruckingStandard flatbedsOften oversize loads that need permits, escorts and set travel windows
Risk in transitLow: no finishes or systems installed yetRoad vibration and lifting can stress connections, finishes, plumbing and electrical
Setting on siteBolt-together frame, no on-site weldsUsually a large crane to set and stitch the modules together
Room sizes & spansClear spans up to 100'Limited by how wide a module can go down the road, with seams where modules meet
ResultOne permanent, 100+ year steel buildingJoined modules
ApprovalsDSA pre-approved plansVaries by manufacturer

Modular quality varies by manufacturer. These are common trade-offs of shipping finished modules, not claims about any specific product.

01 · Built Strong

A steel moment frame
at its core.

Engineered for California

The SidePlate® moment frame bolts together with zero on-site welds and exceeds seismic and wind-shear code — in more than 40 full-scale third-party tests, sometimes by as much as double. Rated for 135 mph winds, a CUUBE can serve as a community place of refuge during and after a disaster.
